Wenbo (Evan) Wu is a PhD Candidate at University of Southampton working under supervision of Prof. George Konstantinidis and Dr. Luis-Daniel Ibáñez.

He completed his MSc study at the University of Bristol, where he obtained his MSc degree in Communication Networks and Signal Processing in 2022.

Prior to his MSc, he got his BEng degree (information security) from Xidian University in 2020.
